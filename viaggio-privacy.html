VIAGGIO: Trip Organizer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. The short version

VIAGGIO has no accounts and no sign-up. Your trips are stored on your device, not on our servers. To build or edit a trip, though, the app does have to send what you asked for to our server, and our server passes it to an AI provider. That is described in full below, because it is the one place your data leaves your phone.

2. What is sent off your device, and when

Nothing is sent in the background. Data leaves your device only when you take one of these actions:

  • Planning a trip. We send your trip brief - destination, dates, number of travellers, budget level and the interests you picked.
  • Editing a trip with the AI planner. We send your current itinerary, the instruction you typed, and the recent messages in that conversation.
  • Asking for suggestions. We send your current itinerary.
  • Uploading a booking screenshot. We send the image or images you selected, plus the dates of your itinerary, so the booking can be placed on the right day. Only the images you explicitly choose are ever uploaded. VIAGGIO does not read your photo library.
  • Importing a plan you already wrote. We send the text you pasted, and any screenshots you attached.

3. Who processes it

The data described above is sent to our own server (hosted on Vercel) and forwarded to Anthropic, whose Claude models generate and edit the itinerary. It is processed to answer your request and the result is returned to your device.

Our server is stateless: it does not have a database, and it does not store your trips, your messages or your screenshots. Anthropic handles the data it receives under its own privacy policy, which you can read at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Standard server request logs (such as timestamps and error information) may be retained briefly by our hosting provider for reliability and abuse prevention.

4. Other services the app talks to

  • Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap and CARTO when you open the Map tab. They receive the tile requests needed to draw the map.
  • Place photos shown on suggestion cards are fetched from Wikipedia.
  • Navigation and booking links open Waze, Google Maps, Booking.com or GetYourGuide in your browser or in those apps. Once you leave VIAGGIO, those companies' own privacy policies apply.

5. What we do not collect

  • No account, no email address, no password, no name.
  • No device location. VIAGGIO never asks for location permission.
  • No contacts, no calendar, no photo-library access.
  • No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no third-party trackers.
  • No advertising identifier, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • Your data is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

6. Where your trips live

Your saved trips - including everything the AI generated for them - are stored locally on your device. Deleting a trip in the app removes it from your device. Deleting the app removes all of it. Because there is no account, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, and trips do not sync between devices.

7. Children

VIAGGIO is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is sent off your device, the updated policy will be published on this page with a new date.
