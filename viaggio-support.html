Getting started

There is no account and no sign-up. When you open VIAGGIO for the first time, a complete four-day Rome itinerary is already loaded so you can see how everything works before planning anything of your own.

  • Today - the countdown to departure and the stops for the selected day. Tap the estimated-total chip at the top for a full cost breakdown.
  • Route - the whole trip as a day-by-day timeline.
  • Map - every stop pinned, with each day's route drawn between the pins.
  • AI - ask for changes in plain language.

To build your own trip, tap the gold + button and enter a destination and dates.

Common questions

Planning failed or timed out. Building a trip needs an internet connection. If it fails, check your connection and try again - nothing is saved until the itinerary comes back.

The AI asked me a question instead of changing anything. That is deliberate. When a request is ambiguous - which day, which stop, how many - VIAGGIO asks rather than guessing. Answer and it will propose the change.

A change I asked for was not applied. Every edit is shown as a preview first. It only takes effect when you tap Apply.

My booking screenshot was not added. The booking's dates have to fall inside your trip's dates. If they do not, VIAGGIO says so instead of putting it on the wrong day. Check the trip dates, and use a clear photo of the confirmation.

Can I delete the Rome example? Yes. Open the trip switcher by tapping the destination name at the top, then delete it. It will not come back.

Do my trips sync between devices? No. Trips are stored on the device. Use the share button to send a plain-text copy of a plan.

How do I delete my data? Deleting a trip removes it from your device; deleting the app removes everything. There is no account, so there is nothing stored about you elsewhere.

Privacy

Planning and editing send your trip brief - and any booking screenshot you choose to upload - to our server for AI processing. This is described in full in the privacy policy.
